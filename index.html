This pattern is common in equipment distribution, where inbound lead flow varies and reps spend significant time identifying and qualifying opportunities. Some companies have found ways to focus this time more effectively.

How Some Companies Reclaimed Time

Brussels-based distributor, 60 employees
Implemented construction permit alerts. Sales team now contacts projects that have already filed permits, rather than searching broadly. Reclaimed 12+ hours weekly per rep.
Antwerp-based distributor, 45 employees
Used LinkedIn to identify job changes among chefs and hotel managers—moments when new vendor evaluations typically begin. Warm outreach replaced cold calling.
Ghent-based distributor, 80 employees
Added a brief qualification step before first meetings. Reps now engage only with prospects who have confirmed budget and timeline. Quote-to-close rate improved significantly.
